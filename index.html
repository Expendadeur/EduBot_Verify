How it works

Three steps to verify any information

Simple enough for first-time users, powerful enough for journalists and educators.

01

Paste the information

Copy any suspicious message, headline or rumor from WhatsApp, Facebook, or any platform and paste it in the app.

02

AI analyzes it

The AI processes the claim in real time and streams a verdict with full reasoning — word by word, in your language.

03

Share responsibly

Armed with the verdict and explanation, you can share responsibly — or choose not to share at all.
